## Rectification log

### PL-062 — My Library keeps no provenance

- **Severity:** Minor. Attribution is imprecise, and `meal_entries.source` misdescribes re-added food. Since PL-060 the notice errs toward Open Food Facts, so no OFF data loses attribution.
- **Reported as:** found in code review during PL-060's investigation (`2026-10-25-internal.md`, PL-060 block, a and b).
- **Root cause:** confirmed in code.
  - `saved_ingredients` has `barcode` and `off_id` but no source and no custom-food link.
  - `saveIngredient` (`src/store/useStore.ts`) runs after EVERY create-path log in ProductScreen, including AI photo estimates and custom foods.
  - `savedIngredientToProduct` (`src/lib/library.ts`) builds a product with no `source` and no `custom_food_id`.
  - ProductScreen then picks the entry's `source` from what's left: `"barcode"` when the library row has a barcode, otherwise `"search"`. So a custom food with a barcode, re-added from My Library, logs as `"barcode"` with no `off_id` and no `custom_food_id`. An AI estimate or a barcode-less custom food logs as `"search"`.
  - Hosted data, 2026-11-18: 79 `barcode` rows with a barcode but no `off_id` or custom link, and 29 `search` rows with neither. Both groups have rows up to this week. Neither group is proven to be custom or AI (verification SQL in PL-060's block). No code path writes OFF data without its `off_id`.
- **Fix:** — Needs a `saved_ingredients` migration that adds provenance, for example a nullable `source` and `custom_food_id`. It has to be written by `saveIngredient` and carried by `savedIngredientToProduct` into the product's source and custom link. Existing library rows can't be backfilled with certainty; they would stay "unknown, treated as OFF".
- **Where:** —
- **Status:** Logged
